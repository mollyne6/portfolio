Attachment Focus

Digital Literacy

Community training, digital inclusion, and practical technology access.

Overview

My digital literacy work centers on helping people build confidence in using everyday digital tools, understanding online systems, and accessing services more effectively. I enjoy making technology feel approachable, especially for learners who are beginning to explore the digital world.

This work connects strongly with my broader goal of improving digital access in communities and helping others use technology to solve real challenges.

Core Skills

Community training Digital access Basic IT support User guidance Technology awareness

What I Support

  • Helping learners navigate computers, mobile devices, and common digital applications.
  • Teaching digital communication, productivity, and online safety fundamentals.
  • Simplifying technology concepts for beginners and community users.
  • Supporting digital inclusion through practical and approachable guidance.

Impact

By improving digital confidence, learners become better prepared for education, online opportunities, and participation in modern community services.
