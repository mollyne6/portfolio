Project Overview

Community Digital Hub Portal

A digital access platform designed to support community learning and technology engagement.

Project Goal

This portal was designed to act as a welcoming digital space for a community technology hub. The goal was to support access to digital literacy resources, promote learning, and present important information in a clear, modern format.

It reflects the importance of creating technology spaces that are easy to understand, accessible, and useful for people of different skill levels.

Tech Stack

HTML CSS JavaScript

What the Platform Includes

  • Community-focused content structure for digital learning.
  • Clean, user-friendly interface for information presentation.
  • Simple navigation for visitors accessing support and learning resources.
  • Visual style aligned with a modern and inclusive community digital environment.

Impact

The project demonstrates how web design can help communities access information, build digital confidence, and strengthen engagement with local technology services.
